Pretty limited options at breakfast time. Collegetown Bagels opens at 6:30am daily (though they typically reduce hours during breaks) and 7-Eleven is 24/7.

Dos Amigos added breakfast a few months ago, but only Wednesday through Sunday, and not until 9am. Fresh Marche, formerly Chatty Cathy, also opens daily at 9.
