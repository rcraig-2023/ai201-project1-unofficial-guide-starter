We actually first launched the reusable takeout container program back in the spring of 2017, starting with Okenshields, expanding to the West Campus dining rooms, and then North Campus dining rooms. When the pandemic hit, New York State Department of Health restrictions had us temporarily stop the program, but we were able to reintroduce it before long.

Our original green "Eco-Takeout" containers had a $7.50 fee at first. The current $5 fee is going up to $6 on August 1st.

1y ago
When you're eating a meal in the residential (all-you-care-to-eat) dining rooms, you're welcome to take something like a piece of fruit, a cookie, or an ice cream cone with you when you leave.

If you'd like to take your meal to go to eat elsewhere, we also have a reusable takeout container program in partnership with Fill It Forward so as to be sustainable, vs. offering disposable containers that'll end up in the landfill. Want to eat in the dining room and take a second meal to go to eat later? No problem! Just tell the door checker that and use two meal swipes.

Depending on where you'll be living and how many long-hour days you'll be on campus, we have unlimited meal plans and smaller meal plans, including specific plan offerings for grad students. You can also just pay at the door per meal if you're just doing that once in a while.

Good luck with your travels! We'll see you in a few weeks.
